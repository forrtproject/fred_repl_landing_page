Where the data comes from

The Replication Atlas is a search layer over FLoRA, FORRT's Library of Reproduction and Replication Attempts, which grows out of the FORRT Replication Database (FReD). It currently covers __PAPER_COUNT__+ original findings paired with replication and reproduction attempts across research disciplines.

Coverage is not complete, and it is not meant to be read as a verdict on any single paper. If a replication is missing, or a record looks wrong, sending it in is the fastest way to get it fixed.
